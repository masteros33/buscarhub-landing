One sale writes every record it touches

A cashier taps three items and takes the cash. That writes the stock movements, posts a balanced journal, updates what the customer owes and lands in the owner's overview — all at once, with nothing to tidy up afterwards.

1Nothing is typed twiceThe till sends what was bought, never what it costs. Prices, stock and discounts are worked out in one place, so a cashier cannot change them.
2Two tills can't oversellA product is held for one till while its sale is written, so the last one on the shelf is sold once and only once.
3History stays historyEvery line keeps the price it sold at. Changing a price today never rewrites what was sold yesterday.
4Tax follows the dateEvery rate carries the day it starts. A new rate begins on its day and the old one ends the day before, so old receipts stay correct.

The till sells with no internet

The product list and settings are cached on each device, so searching and selling carry on with no connection. Sales made offline wait on that device and go up when the network returns.

  • Never duplicated. Every sale carries its own mark, so sending it twice can never make two.
  • Never refused. An offline sale is not blocked for low stock or a changed price. It is recorded, and anything unusual is flagged for a manager.
  • Survives a power cut. The till keeps its own copy on the device, so after a restart it opens straight to selling.
  • Tied to the cashier. Queued sales belong to whoever made them, and signing out is blocked while any are waiting.

What it is, plainly

  • DatabaseYour own database, kept apart from every other business. Nobody else’s records sit beside yours.
  • StockEvery movement in and out is kept, and nothing is written over. Two tills can never both sell the last one.
  • AccountsEvery sale, payment and expense writes both sides of the books at once. Anything that does not balance is refused.
  • TaxVAT, NHIL and GETFund, each with the date it starts. A new rate begins on its day and the old one ends the day before.
  • PayrollGRA monthly PAYE bands, SSNIT 5.5% and 13%, Tier 1 13.5%, Tier 2 5%, insurable earnings capped.
  • MessagingTexts go out through Arkesel or Hubtel, with delivery reports and a monthly allowance.
  • PaymentsPaystack payment links on unpaid invoices, recorded only once Paystack confirms the money.
  • BackupsA full copy of your records every morning, kept away from the machine running the system, and the last 30 mornings are held.
  • RolesOwner, manager, cashier, storekeeper, accountant, waiter, kitchen — and the owner can build more.
