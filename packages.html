The packages

Shop

For shops, supermarkets, pharmacies, wholesalers and anyone who sells over a counter.

  • The till, with split payments, holds and receipts that name the branch
  • Stock across branches, counts, transfers and the reorder planner
  • Buying from suppliers, customers on credit and statements
  • Expenses, daily close, accounts, bank and cash
  • Keeps selling with no internet
  • Staff clocking in and leave

HRM

For any business with staff, with or without a till.

  • One record for each person: job, pay details, next of kin
  • Clocking in and out, lateness and absence
  • Leave asked for, approved or refused, counted in working days
  • Ghana's public holidays, never counted as leave
  • Who is away when, and how much leave each person has left
  • Bring the staff list in from a spreadsheet

Payroll

For the person who pays salaries every month.

  • PAYE by the GRA's bands and SSNIT 5.5% and 13%, Tier 1 and Tier 2
  • Your own allowances and deductions, with the usual Ghanaian ones ready
  • Provident fund with the business's share, bonus at 5%, overtime
  • Loans taken back month by month
  • Payslips laid out as Ghanaian payslips are, which staff see for themselves
  • PAYE and SSNIT schedules, and what is still to be sent

CRM

For businesses that win customers before they sell to them.

  • Leads, turned into a customer and a deal in one step
  • Deals on a board by step, with what each is worth
  • Calls and visits with a day and a name, late ones marked
  • The people at each customer
  • Each customer's whole history on one page, purchases included
  • Deals going cold, and why deals are lost
coming soon

Logbook

For gates, offices and yards that keep a log.

  • Visitors signed in and out
  • Vehicles and deliveries at the gate
  • Keys and equipment: who has what
  • Incidents, and the duty officer's shift log
  • A record that cannot be changed afterwards
